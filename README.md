# 🚀 AI Career Navigator

An AI-powered career guidance platform that analyzes resumes, extracts candidate skills, recommends suitable job roles, and matches users with relevant job opportunities collected from live job portals.

---

## 📌 Overview

Finding the right job can be challenging, especially when candidates are unsure which roles best match their skills. AI Career Navigator simplifies this process by automatically analyzing a user's resume, identifying technical skills, recommending suitable career paths, and matching them with relevant job opportunities.

The application also calculates a resume score, helping users understand how well their resume aligns with industry expectations.

## 🧠 How It Works

### 1. Resume Upload
Users upload their resume in PDF format.

### 2. Resume Parsing
The application extracts text from the uploaded resume.

### 3. Skill Extraction
Technical skills are identified from the extracted content.

### 4. Resume Analysis
The system evaluates the resume and generates a resume score.

### 5. Career Recommendation
Suitable job roles are recommended based on the extracted skills.

### 6. Live Job Collection
Relevant jobs are collected dynamically from RemoteOK.

### 7. Job Matching
Jobs are ranked according to the similarity between required skills and the candidate's skills.

### 8. Dashboard
The best matching opportunities are displayed through an interactive web interface.
